# Helios Cloud Billing and Refunds

Helios Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Team, and Enterprise. Starter costs $19 per month, Team costs $79 per month, and Enterprise pricing is negotiated per contract.

Annual subscriptions receive a 15% discount compared with monthly billing. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are payable within 30 days.

New customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. After that window, refunds are only issued for verified billing errors. Refund requests must be sent to the billing team through the account dashboard.

Usage above the plan quota is billed at $0.04 per additional gigabyte of storage per month. Overage charges appear on the next invoice.
