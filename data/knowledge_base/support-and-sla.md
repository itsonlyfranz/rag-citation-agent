# Helios Cloud Support and SLA

Helios Cloud guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime for Team and Enterprise plans. Starter plans have no uptime guarantee.

If monthly uptime falls below the guaranteed level, affected customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee. Uptime below 99.0% earns a credit of 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident.

Email support is available on all plans, with a first response within 2 business days on Starter and within 8 business hours on Team. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated support engineer and a first response within 1 hour for critical incidents.

Phone support is not offered on any plan. Status updates are published on the public status page during incidents.
