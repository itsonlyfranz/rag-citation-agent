# Helios Cloud Security and Data Retention

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days.

Audit logs are retained for 90 days on the Team plan and for 400 days on the Enterprise plan. Starter plan customers do not have access to audit logs.

Deleted projects are kept in a recovery bin for 30 days and are then permanently erased. Backups are retained for 35 days after the original data is deleted.

Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 is available on the Enterprise plan only. Multi-factor authentication is available on every plan and is mandatory for administrator accounts.
